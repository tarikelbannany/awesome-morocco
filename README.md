## Central Government Portals

- **[Idarati](https://idarati.ma/)** — National portal of administrative procedures and formalities. Single digital interface for all government services. Hotline: **3737**. Upgraded to **Idarati X.0** in February 2026 with AI and secure digital identity.
- **[Maroc.ma](https://www.maroc.ma/en)** — Official national portal of Morocco. Gateway to government institutions, news, and all e-services.
- **[Chikaya](https://www.chikaya.ma/)** — National complaints and suggestions portal for reporting issues with public services and territorial collectivities.
- **[SGG — Secrétariat Général du Gouvernement](https://www.sgg.gov.ma/)** — General Secretariat of the Government. Access to the Official Bulletin (Bulletin Officiel), legislation, and regulatory texts.
- **[MAEC — Diplomatie](https://www.diplomatie.ma/)** — Ministry of Foreign Affairs, African Cooperation and Moroccans Abroad. Diplomacy and consular network.

## Digital Identity & Civil Status

- **[CINe — Carte Nationale d'Identité Électronique](https://www.cnie.ma)** — National Electronic Identity Card portal. Application and renewal of the biometric eCard.
- **[Passeport.ma](https://www.passeport.ma/)** — National Electronic Passport portal. Application and renewal of the biometric passport.
- **[Watiqa](https://www.watiqa.ma/)** — Online ordering of administrative documents (birth certificates, extracts, death certificates) with delivery by registered mail.
- **[Wraqi](https://wraqi.ma/)** — Digital wallet for paperless, secure e-government documents. Part of the #DigitalMorocco2030 initiative, integrated with national digital identity.
- **[Mon Identité Numérique](https://www.identitenumerique.ma/)** — National digital identity service. Single secure login for online public services.
- **[RNP](https://www.rnp.ma/)** — National Population Registry. Unique civil digital ID (IDCN) underpinning e-government services.
- **[Certificat de Nationalité](https://nationalitemarocaine.justice.gov.ma/)** — Online request for the Moroccan nationality certificate (Ministry of Justice).

## Administration & Public Services

- **[Rokhas](https://rokhas.ma/)** — National one-stop shop for all territorial authorizations (construction permits, economic activity permits).
- **[Casier Judiciaire en ligne](https://casierjudiciaire.justice.gov.ma/)** — Online criminal record extract request. Delivery via email (with CINe), registered mail, or court pickup. QR code verification.
- **[E-Police — DGSN](https://www.epolice.ma/)** — Digital portal for police administrative services: CINe appointments, police recruitment exams, anthropometric records.
- **[Médiateur du Royaume](https://www.mediateur.ma/)** — Ombudsman institution. Submit and track administrative complaints against public services.
- **[Bureau d'Ordre Digital](https://bodigital.gov.ma/)** — Digital mail registry. Submit and track official correspondence to any administration online.

## Finance & Tax

- **[SIMPL — DGI Tax Portal](https://simpl.tax.gov.ma/)** — Online tax declaration and payment platform (VAT, IS, IR). Managed by the Direction Générale des Impôts.
- **[DGI Portal](https://www.tax.gov.ma/)** — General Directorate of Taxes main portal. Legislation, regulations, real estate price references, and SIMPL teleservices.
- **[Daribati (Mobile App)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ma.tax.dgi.daribati)** — DGI mobile app for tax services on the go.
- **[TGR — Trésorerie Générale du Royaume](https://www.tgr.gov.ma/)** — General Treasury of the Kingdom. Online payment of local taxes (housing tax, municipal services tax, professional tax).
- **[TGR eServices](https://eservices.tgr.gov.ma/)** — Dedicated e-services platform of the General Treasury.
- **[Foreign Exchange Office](https://www.oc.gov.ma)** — Office des Changes. Regulation and supervision of foreign exchange operations. Interactive foreign trade database.
- **[Vignette.ma](https://www.vignette.ma/)** — Online payment of the annual special vehicle tax (TSAVA / vignette).
- **[Timbre Fiscal](https://timbre-fiscal.tax.gov.ma/)** — Electronic fiscal stamp (e-timbre) purchase and verification (DGI).
- **[Ministère de l'Économie et des Finances](https://www.finances.gov.ma/)** — Ministry of Economy and Finance. Budget, public finance, economic policy and financial regulations.

## Banking & Capital Markets

- **[Bank Al-Maghrib](https://www.bkam.ma/)** — Central Bank of Morocco. Monetary policy, banking supervision, and financial statistics.
- **[AMMC](https://www.ammc.ma/)** — Autorité Marocaine du Marché des Capitaux. Capital market authority and regulation.
- **[Bourse de Casablanca](https://www.casablanca-bourse.com/)** — Casablanca Stock Exchange. Second-largest in Africa. Real-time market data, investor portal, and listed companies (78 companies, ~1 trillion MAD market cap).
- **[Al Barid Bank](https://www.albaridbank.ma/)** — Postal bank with 1,900+ branches. Online banking and Barid Bank Mobile app for digital financial services.
- **[CDG](https://www.cdg.ma/)** — Caisse de Dépôt et de Gestion. Public financial institution managing savings, pensions and strategic investments.
- **[Casablanca Finance City](https://casablancafinancecity.com/)** — Africa's leading financial hub and gateway for regional investment.
- **[Crédit Agricole du Maroc](https://www.creditagricole.ma/)** — Public bank financing agriculture, rural development and green projects.

## Social Security & Health Insurance

- **[CNSS](https://www.cnss.ma/)** — Caisse Nationale de Sécurité Sociale. Social security fund for the private sector.
- **[MaCNSS](https://www.macnss.ma/)** — CNSS online services portal for employers, employees, and beneficiaries. Salary declarations, file tracking, certificate generation, pension simulation.
- **[Damancom](https://damancom.cnss.ma/)** — CNSS employer portal for employee declarations and social contributions. Secured with digital identity (e-ID) and OTP authentication.
- **[CNOPS](https://www.cnops.org.ma/)** — Caisse Nationale des Organismes de Prévoyance Sociale. Health insurance for the public sector.
- **[ACAPS](https://www.acaps.ma/)** — Insurance and Social Welfare Supervisory Authority. Information on AMO (mandatory health insurance), automobile insurance, and social protection. Online complaint filing.
- **[RSU — Registre Social Unifié](https://www.rsu.ma/)** — Unified Social Register. Required registration for accessing social aid programs (AMO Tadamon, direct social support, Tayssir).
- **[CMR](https://www.cmr.gov.ma/)** — Moroccan Pension Fund. Pensions for public and para-public employees. Online account and simulation.
- **[RCAR](https://www.rcar.ma/)** — Collective Retirement Allowance Scheme for contract and semi-public staff (managed by CDG).
- **[CNRA](https://www.cnra.ma/)** — National Fund for Retirement and Insurance. Annuities and supplementary retirement products (CDG Prévoyance).

## Health

- **[Ministry of Health Portal](https://www.sante.gov.ma/)** — Official portal of the Ministry of Health and Social Protection.
- **[Liqahcorona.ma](https://www.liqahcorona.ma/)** — National COVID-19 vaccination management portal and registry.

## Education & Training

- **[Massar — Moutamadris](https://massar.men.gov.ma/)** — Ministry of Education student portal. Grades, absences, homework, continuous assessments, and school career tracking. Mobile app available.
- **[Taalim.ma](https://taalim.ma/)** — Ministry of National Education platform. Student and teacher email accounts, Microsoft Office integration.
- **[Tayssir](https://tayssir.cdgprevoyance.ma/)** — Conditional cash transfer program for education. Registration and tracking for families receiving school attendance allowances (2.3 million children).
- **[OFPPT](https://www.ofppt.ma/)** — Office of Vocational Training and Employment Promotion. Free vocational training across 370 centers, 187 diploma programs, 500,000+ trainees.
- **[MarMOOC](https://marmooc.ma/)** — First Moroccan e-learning platform (MOOCs). Collaboration between six public universities.
- **[Campus Virtuel Marocain](https://cev.uca.ma/)** — Moroccan Virtual Campus for complementary e-learning content in higher education.
- **[Minhaty](https://www.minhaty.ma/)** — National university scholarship portal (ONOUSC). RSU-based eligibility, application and tracking.
- **[ONOUSC](https://www.onousc.ma/)** — National Office of University, Social and Cultural Works. Student housing, meals and scholarships.
- **[Cursussup](https://www.cursussup.gov.ma/)** — National platform for online enrollment in public higher-education institutions.
- **[E-Tawjihi](https://e-tawjihi.ma/)** — Orientation and admission platform for Moroccan higher schools and universities.
- **[MyWay](https://www.myway.ac.ma/)** — Academic and career guidance platform helping students choose their path.
- **[CPGE](https://www.cpge.ac.ma/)** — Preparatory classes for grandes écoles. Online application and selection.
- **[E-Madrassa](https://e-madrassa.ma/)** — National e-learning platform. Digital lessons and resources for school pupils.
- **[Ministère de l'Éducation Nationale](https://www.men.gov.ma/)** — Ministry of National Education, Preschool and Sports. Policies, exams and the Massar ecosystem.
- **[Enseignement Supérieur](https://www.enssup.gov.ma/)** — Ministry of Higher Education, Scientific Research and Innovation. Universities and research policy.

## Research & Innovation

- **[CNRST](https://www.cnrst.ma/)** — National Centre for Scientific and Technical Research. Coordination of scientific research and innovation in Morocco.
- **[PRSM — Portail des Revues Scientifiques Marocaines](https://revues.imist.ma/)** — National portal for Moroccan scientific journals. Open-access publishing, peer review, and article submission managed by IMIST/CNRST.

## Employment & Social Aid

- **[ANAPEC](https://anapec.ma/)** — National Agency for Employment and Skills Promotion. Job search, recruitment, and employment programs (IDMAJ, TAHFIZ, KAFAAT). 75 branches nationwide.
- **[Entraide Nationale](https://www.entraide.ma/)** — National Mutual Aid. Social programs for vulnerable populations.
- **[Ministry of Solidarity](https://social.gov.ma/)** — Ministry of Solidarity, Social Development, Equality and Family. Social aid programs directory.
- **[Emploi-Public](https://www.emploi-public.ma/)** — National public-sector recruitment portal. Job offers and competitive exams across ministries.
- **[INDH](https://www.indh.ma/)** — National Initiative for Human Development. Programs against poverty and social exclusion.
- **[Fondation Mohammed V — Solidarité](https://www.fm5.ma/)** — Mohammed V Foundation for Solidarity. Social programs, training centers and poverty relief.

## Housing & Urban Planning

- **[Daam Sakane](https://www.daamsakane.ma/)** — Direct housing support portal. Online registration and tracking for government housing aid (up to 100,000 DH). Full digital process, 2024-2028 program.
- **[Al Omrane](https://www.alomrane.gov.ma/)** — Public real estate development group. Online property search, credit simulation, and appointment scheduling across 550+ projects.
- **[Géoportail Urbanisme](https://www.taamir.gov.ma/karazal/)** — Urban planning geoportal. Access to urban planning documents for the entire Kingdom.

## Justice & Legal

- **[Casier Judiciaire](https://casierjudiciaire.justice.gov.ma/)** — Online criminal record request portal with QR code verification and multi-channel delivery.
- **[Ministry of Justice](https://www.justice.gov.ma/)** — Official portal of the Ministry of Justice. Legal information and judicial services.
- **[Mahakim.ma](https://mahakim.ma/)** — Judicial case tracking and court information system.
- **[Adala](https://adala.justice.gov.ma/)** — Legal database of the Ministry of Justice. Laws, jurisprudence and legal publications.
- **[CSPJ](https://www.cspj.ma/)** — Superior Council of the Judicial Power. Independent governance of judges and the judiciary.

## Police & Security

- **[E-Police — DGSN](https://www.epolice.ma/)** — Digital police services portal. CINe appointments, police recruitment, anthropometric records, criminal record requests (for MRE). 34,000+ requests processed in 2025.
- **[DGSN](https://www.dgsn.gov.ma/)** — General Directorate of National Security portal.

## Transport & Road Safety

- **[NARSA Khadamat](https://khadamat.narsa.gov.ma/)** — National Road Safety Agency online services. Appointments, driving license applications, and vehicle registration (carte grise).
- **[NARSA — Consultation PC/CG](http://consultpccg.narsa.gov.ma/)** — Online verification and tracking of driving licenses and vehicle registration cards.
- **[Ministry of Transport](https://www.equipement.gov.ma/)** — Ministry of Equipment and Water / Transport and Logistics portal.
- **[Infractions Routières](https://infractionsroutieres.narsa.gov.ma/)** — Consult and pay road traffic fines online (NARSA).
- **[ADM — Autoroutes du Maroc](https://www.adm.co.ma/)** — National highways. Toll network, live traffic (ADM Trafic) and the Jawaz electronic toll pass.
- **[AMDL](https://www.amdl.gov.ma/)** — Moroccan Agency for Logistics Development. National logistics strategy and freight platforms.

## Airports & Railways

- **[ONCF](https://www.oncf.ma/)** — National Railways Office. Online train ticket booking (Al Boraq high-speed, regular lines), schedules, and digital tickets with QR codes.
- **[ONDA](https://www.onda.ma/)** — National Airports Office. 25 airports (19 international). Passenger services, flight info, and digital check-in/baggage systems.
- **[Royal Air Maroc](https://www.royalairmaroc.com/)** — National flag carrier. Flight booking, check-in and the Safar Flyer loyalty program.

## Postal Services

- **[Poste Maroc — Barid Al-Maghrib](https://www.poste.ma/)** — National postal service. Mail, parcels, express delivery, money transfers, and bill payments.
- **[Barid Al-Maghrib Tracking](https://bam-tracking.barid.ma/)** — Online parcel and shipment tracking portal.

## Business & Investment

- **[OMPIC — Business Creation Platform](http://www.ompic.ma/)** — Moroccan Office of Industrial and Commercial Property. Online business registration, name reservation, trademark/patent protection, and central trade register. 19,000+ companies created online in 2025.
- **[DirectInfo](http://www.directinfo.ma/)** — OMPIC's business registry search. Free company lookup by name or RC number. Legal and financial information on registered enterprises.
- **[CRI-Invest](https://www.cri-invest.ma/)** — Regional Investment Centers. One-stop shop for company incorporation, investment support, and business assistance.
- **[Maroc PME](https://www.marocpme.gov.ma/)** — National SME Agency. Support programs, financing, and digital transformation for small and medium enterprises.
- **[Morocco Foodex (EACCE)](https://www.moroccofoodex.org.ma/)** — Export control and coordination. Promotion of Moroccan food exports and quality certification.
- **[AMDIE](https://www.amdie.gov.ma/)** — Moroccan Agency for Investment and Export Development. Investor support, incentives and export promotion.
- **[DirectEntreprise](https://www.directentreprise.ma/)** — Official platform for company information and legal publications from the business registry.
- **[Ministère de l'Industrie et du Commerce](https://www.mcinet.gov.ma/)** — Ministry of Industry and Trade. Industrial strategy, trade policy and the industrial project bank.

## Customs & Foreign Trade

- **[ADII — Customs](https://www.douane.gov.ma/)** — Administration of Customs and Indirect Taxes portal.
- **[BADR](https://badr.douane.gov.ma/)** — Automated Base for Networked Customs. Electronic customs clearance system for import/export (declarations, risk analysis, tariff liquidation, payment).
- **[PortNet](https://www.portnet.ma/)** — National Single Window for foreign trade procedures. 120+ online services, 53,000+ users. Simplification and digitalization of international trade.
- **[ANP](https://www.anp.org.ma/)** — National Ports Agency. Regulation and management of Morocco's commercial and fishing ports.
- **[Marsa Maroc](https://www.marsamaroc.co.ma/)** — Leading port terminal operator handling container and bulk cargo.
- **[Tanger Med](https://www.tangermed.ma/)** — Tanger Med port complex. Largest port in the Mediterranean and Africa; industrial platform.

## Public Procurement

- **[Marchés Publics](https://www.marchespublics.gov.ma/)** — Moroccan Public Procurement Portal. Tender notices, bid document downloads, and electronic submissions. Managed by the TGR. 50,000+ notices/year.

## Land Registry & Real Estate

- **[ANCFCC](https://www.ancfcc.gov.ma/)** — National Agency for Land Conservation, Cadastre and Cartography. Land titling, property certificates, and cadastral records.
- **[Mohafadati](https://mohafadati.ancfcc.gov.ma/)** — Digital tracking of land titles and administrative formalities. Secure monitoring of property-related procedures.

## Energy & Water

- **[ONEE](https://www.onee.ma/)** — National Office of Electricity and Drinking Water. Electricity and water services, bill management.
- **[MASEN](https://www.masen.ma/)** — Moroccan Agency for Sustainable Energy. Solar, wind, and hydropower project development (Noor Plan). Target: 6,000 MW by 2030.
- **[IRESEN](https://www.iresen.org/)** — Research Institute for Solar Energy and New Energies. R&D in renewable energy and green hydrogen.
- **[ONHYM](https://www.onhym.com/)** — National Office of Hydrocarbons and Mines. Exploration data, mining permits and investor portal.
- **[AMEE](https://www.amee.ma/)** — Moroccan Agency for Energy Efficiency. Programs and standards to reduce energy consumption.
- **[ANRE](https://anre.ma/)** — National Electricity Regulatory Authority. Grid access, tariffs and electricity market liberalization.
- **[LYDEC](https://www.lydec.ma/)** — Casablanca water, electricity and sanitation distributor. Online billing and account management.
- **[Amendis](https://www.amendis.ma/)** — Water, electricity and sanitation distributor for the Tangier and Tetouan regions.
- **[Redal](https://www.redal.ma/)** — Water, electricity and sanitation distributor for the Rabat-Salé region.
- **[Fatourati](https://www.fatourati.ma/)** — Online bill-payment platform for utility invoices (water, electricity and more).

## Agriculture & Rural Development

- **[ADA](https://www.ada.gov.ma/)** — Agency for Agricultural Development. Agricultural plans, market access, government support, and social agriculture programs.
- **[ONSSA](https://www.onssa.gov.ma/)** — National Office of Food Safety. Sanitary controls, establishment registration (Atlas platform), and online payments.
- **[Fila7a.com](https://fila7a.com/)** — First mobile application for agriculture in Morocco. Community platform for farmers, buyers, and sellers of agricultural products.

## Fisheries & Aquaculture

- **[ANDA — Agence Nationale pour le Développement de l'Aquaculture](https://anda.gov.ma/)** — National Aquaculture Development Agency. Investor space, project support, and licensing.
- **[ANDA GeoPortail](https://geoportail.anda.gov.ma/)** — Geographic portal for aquaculture. Maps of active farms, available parcels, and landing infrastructures.
- **[INRH](https://www.inrh.ma/)** — National Institute of Fisheries Research. Marine ecosystem studies, resource assessment, and aquaculture research.

## Environment & Forests

- **[Ministry of Energy Transition](https://www.mem.gov.ma/)** — Ministry of Energy Transition and Sustainable Development. Environmental policy and climate change.

## Telecommunications & Digital

- **[ANRT](https://www.anrt.ma/)** — National Telecommunications Regulatory Agency. Licensing, equipment approvals, .ma domain names, electronic certification, and e-services.
- **[ADD](https://www.add.gov.ma/)** — Agency for Digital Development. Digital strategy implementation (Digital Morocco 2030), e-government promotion, and digital ecosystem structuring.
- **[DGSSI](https://www.dgssi.gov.ma/)** — General Directorate of Information Systems Security. National cybersecurity authority (maCERT).

## Data Protection & Privacy

- **[CNDP](https://www.cndp.ma/)** — Commission Nationale de Contrôle de la Protection des Données à Caractère Personnel. Morocco's data protection authority (Law 09-08). Complaint hotline: **3020**.

## Youth & Sports

- **[Ministry of Youth, Culture and Communication](https://mjcc.gov.ma/)** — Official ministry portal. Programs for youth houses, associations, and cultural/sports initiatives.

- **[MAP](https://www.mapnews.ma/)** — Maghreb Arabe Presse. Official state news agency of Morocco.

- **[FNM](https://fnm.ma/)** — National Foundation of Museums. Manages and promotes Morocco's national museums.
- **[IRCAM](https://www.ircam.ma/)** — Royal Institute of Amazigh Culture. Standardization, teaching and promotion of the Amazigh language.

## Religious Affairs

## Local Government & Territorial Collectivities

- **[Portail des Collectivités Territoriales](https://www.collectivites-territoriales.gov.ma/)** — National portal for local government. Services for elected officials, citizens, and businesses across 12 regions, 75 prefectures/provinces, and 1,503 municipalities.

- **[HACA](https://www.haca.ma/)** — High Authority for Audiovisual Communication. Regulation and pluralism of broadcast media.
- **[Conseil de la Concurrence](https://conseil-concurrence.ma/)** — Competition Council. Independent authority regulating markets, mergers and anti-competitive practices.
- **[INPPLC](https://www.inpplc.ma/)** — National Authority for Probity, Prevention and Fighting Corruption.

- **[Morocco GeoPortal](https://morocco.africageoportal.com/)** — Geographic data portal. Maps, satellite imagery, and spatial data powered by Esri.

## Notes

- 🔍 **Language Support**: Most platforms support **Arabic** and **French**. Some also offer **English** and **Tamazight**.
- 📱 **Mobile Apps**: Many services offer companion mobile apps (MaCNSS, Daribati, Massar Moutamadris, TGR Mobile, Casier Judiciaire, Chikaya, Daam Sakane, ONP Mobile, Pass Jeunes, ONCF).
- 🆔 **Digital Identity**: An increasing number of services require the new electronic CINe for authentication.
- 🔄 **Idarati X.0**: Launched in February 2026, this next-generation platform uses AI and secure digital identity to unify all public services into a single interface.
- 📊 **Scale**: Morocco's national digital services registry hosts **600+ online services** for citizens and **200+ for businesses**.
